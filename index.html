Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a rooted device?
No, FreizeDNS uses Android's local VPN service API to route DNS queries without requiring root access. It's completely safe and works out of the box on standard devices.
Why does Android show a VPN icon when FreizeDNS is active?
Android requires apps to create a local VPN interface in order to intercept DNS requests, block IPv6 leaks, and run the built-in firewall without root. Your regular traffic is not routed through a remote VPN server—only your DNS queries are encrypted.
How do I install FreizeDNS on Android TV or Google TV?
Download the Universal APK (or ARM32 for older TV boxes) and transfer it to your TV via a USB drive, Send Files to TV, or a TV browser/Downloader app. The interface automatically adapts to wide screens and remote control navigation.
Which APK architecture should I download?
If you are unsure, choose Universal—it works on all Android devices. For a smaller file size, pick ARM64 (v8a) for modern phones and tablets, ARM32 (v7a) for older phones and budget TV boxes, or x86_64 for PC emulators.
What is Freize ID and is it required?
Basic encrypted DNS protection works immediately without an account. Entering your Freize ID unlocks all additional features and personalized server settings.
Can I exclude specific apps or home Wi-Fi networks?
Yes. The built-in firewall lets you control internet access per app or exclude apps from DNS filtering, and Trusted Wi-Fi automatically pauses FreizeDNS when you connect to your home network.
How does it affect battery life?
The application is highly optimized and written natively. It consumes a negligible amount of battery and runs efficiently in the background.
