FreizeDNS

Secure DNS Client for Android

A modern, secure, and highly customizable DNS client designed specifically for Android. FreizeDNS empowers you to take full control of your internet privacy by encrypting your DNS queries, effectively preventing ISPs and third parties from tracking your online activity. Seamlessly manage custom routing rules in real time, block malicious domains using local lists, and enjoy a safer, unrestricted web experience—whether you're on cellular data or public Wi-Fi networks.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a rooted device?
No, FreizeDNS uses Android's local VPN service API to route DNS queries without requiring root access. It's completely safe and works out of the box on standard devices.
How does it affect battery life?
The application is highly optimized and written natively. It consumes a negligible amount of battery and runs efficiently in the background.
